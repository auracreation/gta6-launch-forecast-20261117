The release candidate combines two regularized fits. They share log opening viewers and the prior-day baseline. The delivered quantile is the equally weighted mean of their log quantiles. Missing windows contribute a neutral feature. Sub-hour source histories in the final are represented by the supplied whole-hour means to match the analog sampling cadence.
